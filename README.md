# Halo

Music visualizer, synced lyrics and music-video wallpaper for your Mac. Works with Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube and SoundCloud.

## Install

1. Download **Halo-share.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/ResoniqEV/halo/releases/latest) and open it.
2. Drag **Halo** into your Applications folder and double-click it.
3. macOS will say it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store). Click Done, then open
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Halo.
4. Allow control of Music / Spotify and **Audio Recording** when asked. Halo only listens to your music apps to move
   the light with the beat and line videos up. It never uses the microphone and records nothing.

## Updating

Halo checks for updates once a day. To check now: menu bar → Halo → **Check for Updates…**

Something not working? Menu bar → Halo → **Copy Diagnostics**, then paste it in a message to whoever sent you Halo.

Needs macOS 14.2 or later (Liquid Glass needs macOS 26).
